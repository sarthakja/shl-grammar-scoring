The station is alive with rhythmic clatter of trains arriving and departing. The loud speaker announces the next trains, destinations and platform numbers, creating a constant background hum. Passengers hurriedly drag suitcases and bags, some briskly walking to catch their trains, while others wait patiently. Checking their watches or mobile phones on the platform, train pull in with a screech or breaks and a hiss of releasing air. Passengers disembark greeting loved ones with hugs and handshakes, while others both settling into their seats and stowing luggage. Conductors in crisp uniforms stand ready, blowing whistles and Waving flags to signal the train is departure.
