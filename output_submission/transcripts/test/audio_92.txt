In the midst of a flood, the landscape is dramatically altered. Streets and fields are submerged under a thick layer of murky water, which can reach several feet deep. Buildings and trees often appear partially submerged, their lower portions obscure or entirely hidden. Debris such as branches, trash, and even pieces of furniture float on the water surface. Carried along by the strong currents, the skies usually overcast, casting a gloomy gray light over the scene, the floodwaters may ripple and surge unpredictably, and the sound of rushing water and the occasional crash of debris create a constant chaotic noise.
