Recently I visited Mualboal in Sepulion for its stunning beaches and vibrant marine life. The ultimate attraction here is the famous Sardine Run where the thousand of sardines create a mesmerizing spectacle just off the coast. The clear waters are perfect for snorkeling and diving allowing visitors to delve into an un- underwater world teeming with colorful coral and diverse Fish species. Beyond the beach, the town offers a laid-back vibe with local eateries serving delicious Cebuano cuisine. Overall, Malboal is a paradise for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike, making it a memorable memorable
