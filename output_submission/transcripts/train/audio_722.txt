One of my favorite places to visit is the Harry Potter World in Orlando, Florida. This is my favorite place to visit because growing up I was a huge fan of the Harry Potter books and subsequently the Harry Potter films. In the Harry Potter world, you can experience all the things a typical wizard would experience in the actual Harry Potter world. From shopping at Ollivander's for a new wand to visiting Diagon Alley to [UH] engage in different activities and shop for new robes, there's always something to do. What I like most about the park is that there's something for all ages, including children as well as adults who who grew up reading the books. In particular, one of my favorite things to eat or drink at the Harry Potter World is butterbeer. It is absolutely delicious and exactly how I imagine it.
