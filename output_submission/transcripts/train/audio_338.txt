My favorite place to visit is the United States of America and specifically Florida. Getting to Florida is quite simple from London Airport, taking approximately eight hours on a direct flight. Nice and easy to just close your eyes, go to sleep and wake up in a Floridian paradise. The local foods that I tend to enjoy are mostly the desserts such as s'mores when it's cool in the evening Having it around a campfire, two graham crackers, sandwiching together melted marshmallow and Hershey's milk chocolate makes a lovely dessert. Not forgetting also pumpkin pies and other desserts like that as well. A particular season that I love is often in the early spring around March time. When it is just warm but not overly humid and just nice in the water when you're strolling along the beach. Absolutely magical with the palm trees and the dolphins just popping out of the water.
