I recently stayed at a luxurious resort nestled at a pristine beach front. The restaurant featured stunning ocean views from my suspicious room, which was elegantly furnished and equipped with all modern elementaries. Each morning began with a garnet breakfast buffet offering a variety of local and international cuisines. Days were filled with activities such as noddling, paragliding and lounging by the infinity pool. The resort spa provided an oasis of relaxation with its range of treatments, including facial massage perfect for unwinding after a day in the sun. Evenings were equally delightful with fine dining options.
