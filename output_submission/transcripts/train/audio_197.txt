You can hear a crowded market before you can see it. You can hear the cacophony of noises, the clanging of change, the ups and downs as people rifle through their bags, trying to figure out which is the best piece of the bunch. Markets are big. Markets continue to be tight, with many people passing through each other like sardines. Hoping to find the next best deal. You have people selling fruit, people selling produce that their own chickens made. You have people selling their own wares as though they are also somehow artisan. In the mornings you can have vendors coming in restocking. As it gets to a gr- roar throughout the day, peaking probably around three p.m. or four p.m. we're slowly dying down.
