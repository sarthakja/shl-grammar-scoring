The scene of a crowded market would involve lots of people, all ranges of demographics, I'd say all cultures, and they all, in my head at least, going to buy fruit and veg for the market. That's probably the most common good sold. Cheap fruit and veg from home grown markets or in farms I would say you can hear lots of people talking and yelling trying to advertise their low prices. And it would start off pretty quiet in the morning and then as the day goes on and things get cheaper and the prices go down it would get busier and at the end of the day it would be packed. It'd be the most people just either going to with the intention of buying stuff or just to walk around and be in that atmosphere.
