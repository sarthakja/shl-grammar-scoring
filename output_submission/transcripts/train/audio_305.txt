My favorite place to visit is the park. To get to the park I must walk down the lively road and cross a very dangerous railroad track. Once I enter into the park, my journey is filled with the sounds of birds chirping as well as the occasional laughter of children. As I am- approach the park, I can smell the freshly cut grass. and feel the dew drops on the grass. The park itself offers some food such as a small cafe where they serve coffee and hot dogs. One of my favorite places is also the bakery where freshly baked croissant is baked every single day. There's something special about this park. There's a lots of different activities such as playing tennis or playing soccer. The park is most beautiful during the fall, where not many people are crowded in the park.
